# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Administrative staff at a catering company who manage daily operations — client records, event planning, menu creation, vehicle fleet, and order fulfillment. They work at a desk, likely on a laptop or desktop, and use this system throughout their shift. Speed and clarity are critical; they need to find, create, and update records quickly without wading through visual clutter.

## Product Purpose

X-Libra Catering is a management system for a catering business. It enables staff to:
- Maintain a client database with contact information
- Plan and schedule events linked to clients
- Define a catalog of menu items with categories and pricing
- Manage a delivery vehicle fleet
- Create and track orders combining events, vehicles, and menu items with quantity and pricing

Success means staff can complete the full order workflow — from client intake to delivery dispatch — within a single system, with minimal friction and no data re-entry.

## Brand Personality

Profesional, elegante, confiable. The interface should convey competence and care — a tool that feels as polished as the catering service itself. Clean, restrained, with thoughtful details that signal quality without shouting.

## Anti-references

- Overly playful or casual UIs (emoji-heavy, cartoonish icons, bright toy colors)
- Dense, cluttered enterprise dashboards with too many widgets per screen
- Restaurant POS-style bold/colorful interfaces (Toast, Clover) — this is not a point-of-sale
- Heavy shadows, extreme gradients, glassmorphism, or any trend that competes with content
- The "AI SaaS default" — cream backgrounds, purple accents, gradient text, glass cards

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration.** Every element earns its place. No purely decorative flourishes that don't serve the task.
2. **Elegant restraint.** A limited palette, generous whitespace, and consistent rhythm. The design recedes so the data is legible.
3. **Efficiency first.** Frequent actions (filter, search, edit) are one click away. The most common path — viewing and editing records — feels frictionless.
4. **Professional polish.** Precision in spacing, alignment, and typography. Small details (hover states, transitions, focus indicators) signal quality.
5. **Reliability through consistency.** One table pattern, one form pattern, one button hierarchy. Predictable interactions build trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices. No specific compliance level required, but all designs should meet basic contrast and keyboard navigation standards.
